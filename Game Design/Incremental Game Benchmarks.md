# Incremental game benchmarks: design solutions Rocket Raja can test

Research checked 30 September 2026. This document compares six incremental games against Rocket Raja's design questions: older products losing relevance, tapping fading behind passive income, prestige feeling thin, pacing through a replay, offline play, and reasons to return. It is a **mechanic benchmark**, not evidence that any game's retention is caused by one feature or that its exact numbers would work in Rocket Raja. The companion [comparison](Comparison%20and%20Recommendations.md) and [economy audit](Rocket%20Raja%20Economy%20Audit.md) describe Rocket Raja's actual implementation and simulator results; the proposed transfers below require simulation and playtests.

## Evidence rules

- **Developer confirmed** means a developer's game page, support article, or patch note. Game mechanics can change: older patch notes are historical examples, and support articles may lag a live build.
- **Community observed** means a player guide or report. It can reveal a useful pattern or friction, but its formula, platform, and version need verification before use as a balance target.
- **Proposed for Rocket Raja** is our inference. It is not a claim about the source game's outcomes.

## 1. AdVenture Capitalist: make old businesses affect the portfolio

**Developer-confirmed mechanics.** Hyper Hippo's current support says some *Unlocks* intentionally reward ownership in one business by boosting another. Its explicit examples are **2,300 Newspapers boosting Donut Shops on Earth** and **27 Bubonic Mouse Traps boosting Pirate Ship Out Boards in an event**. That is a direct answer to “why buy a small old business?”: the marginal value of the next Newspaper can include a Donut Shop increase even if Newspapers have little direct income. The game also uses per-business Gilding that persists through future runs: Gold gives a permanent ×7.77 boost and later Platinum tiers go higher, funded with Mega Bucks. [Unlock explanation](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/506-unlocks-have-incorrect-descriptions/); [Gilding explanation](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/523-what-is-gilding/); [2026 Gilding update](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/548-gilding-mega-ticket-update/).

Its Angel Investors provide a second layer. The current support article says the first Angel becomes available after **$100 trillion** earned on Earth and publishes the expression `150 × sqrt(lifetime career earnings in quadrillions) − angels spent` for earning Angels. This implies diminishing gains per additional earnings unit. The article does not explain rounding or how the unlock gate interacts with the expression, so it should **not** be used to calculate Rocket Raja rewards. The game's statistics distinguish session earnings, lifetime earnings, resets, Angels, and unlocks, giving players several progress measures. [Angel support article](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/3-how-do-i-get-angels/); [statistics FAQ](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/87-statistics-faq/).

**Proposed for Rocket Raja.** A few authored *cross-family* milestones could make Phuljhari, Anar, and Chakri purchases change portfolio output. For example, reaching a late Phuljhari threshold might strengthen a specific later family, while a whole-stall threshold modestly boosts all families. Show the *target family and total ₹/second change* before purchase; Hyper Hippo has had to publish a support article explaining why an unlock displays another business's name. A family-specific permanent Legacy choice could likewise keep a favorite starter line relevant after reset. The transfer is the **dependency structure**, not AdVenture Capitalist's ×7.77 or Platinum values.

**Tradeoff to test.** A global milestone can turn a formerly optional low-yield purchase into a forced chore. Track the marginal portfolio gain per rupee and the share of purchases spent only to satisfy thresholds. Keep a viable alternative to buying all eight families uniformly. A paid-currency Gilding ladder is not needed to validate the idea. Hyper Hippo's 2026 update removed an intermediate ticket currency because it had only one use and could be overbought; this is a useful warning against adding an extra currency just to support one upgrade path. [Gilding update](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/548-gilding-mega-ticket-update/).

## 2. Tap Titans 2: make prestige a visible new run, then shorten the repeat

**Developer-confirmed mechanics.** Game Hive says it *guides the first prestige at stage 60*. Prestige resets stage, Sword Master, Spell, and Hero levels; grants Relics for persistent Artifacts; and also yields stage-related Skill Points, Raid XP, and a speed-up resource. The confirmation shows the **starting stage after prestige**. The FAQ explicitly explains that Artifact boosts help players regain and exceed their old max stage, and that pushing a little further yields more Relics. This makes the reset's loss, reward, and next-run acceleration legible. [Prestige FAQ](https://gamehive.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-tap-titans-2/faq/75-should-i-prestige-when/).

Game Hive's Advanced Start help says milestones determine a higher starting stage and requires progress through at least half the remaining interval before another prestige. Its inactive progression help says offline movement stops at **99% of the player's highest stage**, so passive time rebuilds the known path while a player must actively push the frontier. The help article also specifies a five-minute inactivity threshold. These are current **support-page claims**, not universal recommendations for a business game. [Advanced Start FAQ](https://gamehive.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-tap-titans-2/faq/77-how-does-advanced-start-work/); [offline FAQ](https://gamehive.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-tap-titans-2/faq/84-why-am-i-not-getting-any-offline-progression/).

**Play-style design.** Game Hive's 2017 v2.0 design post describes separate skill-tree branches for tap and pet, heroes and clan ship, active skills, and inactive advancement. The stated intent was several viable play paths rather than one overpowered branch, with easier respec. This is historical evidence of its design method. A 2025 patch still names distinct tap, pet, Shadow Clone, dagger, and other damage sources, showing that the game retained multiple named routes even though the exact 2017 skill tree changed. [v2.0 design post](https://gamehive.com/blog/tt2-devlog-10-first-look-into-v20/); [2025 patch](https://gamehive.com/blog/devlog-181-version-7-8-0-patch-notes/).

**Proposed for Rocket Raja.** The prestige screen should show three concrete numbers: reputation gained, the effect of the next affordable permanent purchase, and an **estimated rebuild outcome** under a specified play policy. After the first few resets, unlock a small specialization that meaningfully changes an active-tap, passive-portfolio, or skill-window route. Give that choice a respec path. Let offline income recover progress without replacing all active frontier decisions; for example, the next tier's *unlock* could require an active choice or goal, while offline production still contributes cash. A hard 99% cash cap would feel artificial in a business game, so do not copy that rule directly.

**Tradeoff to test.** A reset can be mathematically strong but emotionally flat if all the player sees is the same purchase sequence. Conversely, granting many currencies on every reset can obscure what helped. Start with one visible persistent choice and one obvious fast-rebuild benefit, then measure how many minutes players spend repeating known purchases and whether they correctly predict what persists.

## 3. Idle Miner Tycoon: disclose reset cost and preserve parallel progress

**Developer-confirmed mechanics.** Kolibri's support says Prestige resets **one mine**, gives that mine a higher earnings multiplier, and can be done up to six times. It costs in-game Cash, with the price shown before confirmation; the player retains remaining Cash, Super Cash, and other mines. The article explicitly cautions players to leave enough Cash to rebuild. [Prestige FAQ](https://kolibri-games.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-idle-miner-tycoon/faq/48-how-does-the-prestige-system-work/).

Kolibri also describes a Research Island with skills for production and ad boosts, funded through currencies earned across several continents; Mainland mines have explicit completion objectives and award materials for Super Managers. Short themed event mines award achievement badges with a five-day window in the documented format. These give progress outside any single mine's income number. [Research FAQ](https://kolibri-games.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-idle-miner-tycoon/faq/50-what-are-skills-and-the-research-island/); [Mainland objectives](https://kolibri-games.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-idle-miner-tycoon/faq/44-how-do-the-mainland-mines-work/); [event mine FAQ](https://kolibri-games.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-idle-miner-tycoon/faq/45-how-does-the-event-mine-work/).

**Proposed for Rocket Raja.** Before any reset, preview both the permanent multiplier/perk and the liquidity needed to get useful production running again. If a future version introduces local product resets, retaining the other families would reduce perceived loss, but the present eight-family game likely benefits more from a strong whole-stall reset with clear retained rewards. Add a small set of *run objectives*—such as family diversity, a skill combo, or a tap target—that creates an accomplishment beyond `₹/second` and advances an existing progression currency. An event mine's cadence should be tested as a lightweight local objective before introducing a second map or seasonal economy.

**Tradeoff to test.** A “pay to reset” fee can create a trap if the player cannot rebuild, and a portfolio of parallel systems can diffuse attention. If experimenting with a reset fee, show the post-fee balance and the first few required purchases. Existing Rocket Raja prestige should first be made more legible before expanding reset scope.

## 4. Realm Grinder: make active and passive paths different choices

**Developer-confirmed mechanics.** Divine Games' store description explicitly contrasts **Elves that reward tapping** with **Demons that boost powerful structures** and says factions have distinct play styles. It also names further unlock layers—research, reincarnations, excavations—and describes trophies that matter mechanically because strategies use them to boost upgrades. This is stronger than giving tap and passive production two unrelated sets of ×2 upgrades: the player's affiliation changes what they optimize. [Official store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/610080/Realm_Grinder/).

Its March 2025 patch notes provide two useful course corrections. The developer reduced many time-based requirements, streamlined reincarnation/gem requirements, added “Buy All Buildings” and easier autocast controls, and **aggregated clicks per second into a stat that can receive multiplicative increases**. A separate November 2025 patch says an Elven Luck effect can scale from autoclick power and that an existing effect was extended to building tiers 1 and 11. These are dated version changes, not proof that every current Realm Grinder run has equal tap and idle return. [March 2025 patch](https://steamcommunity.com/games/610080/announcements/detail/497191242656907360); [November 2025 patch](https://steamcommunity.com/games/610080/announcements/detail/630068614159926444).

**Proposed for Rocket Raja.** A post-prestige Legacy specialization could produce three intelligible routes: a *Showman* path that links taps to normal shop production, a *Workshop* path that rewards family diversity, and a *Night Shift* path that improves idle/skill timing. Give each a named mechanic, tradeoff, and respec. An active route must compound with the economy and remain visible in the stat panel; a passive route should avoid requiring constant tapping to be viable. Refresh the weakest old family through a mechanical job or synergy, not simply a huge flat multiplier.

**Tradeoff to test.** Realm Grinder can support long chains of unlocks because it has built a very large RPG-like system over years. Rocket Raja should prototype a *single* consequential branching choice after the existing first prestige. Its 2025 reductions in time gates are a reminder that “more content” is not the same as satisfying pacing; excessive waits can overwhelm the value of an unlock.

## 5. Cell to Singularity: actively fix the incentive to wait

**Developer-confirmed mechanics.** The developer FAQ describes tapping, buying nodes, and a simulation reboot that converts accumulated resources to Metabits for faster later runs and new content. The FAQ predates newer systems, so its exact trigger sequence should not be treated as the current live flow. [Developer FAQ](https://celltosingularity.com/faq/).

The team's May 2026 announcement is a more specific balance case. In response to player behavior where **waiting for long periods was more advantageous**, it changed Metabit and Knowledge multipliers to affect all converted currency in the current simulation, making completed Time Chamber fills more valuable. It simultaneously cut two Time Chamber bonus tiers from ×10 to ×5 to control the resulting power. The important mechanism is *retroactive value from an active upgrade*: a player who upgrades now does not lose value on rewards already converted this run. [Official May 2026 announcement, “Metabit & Knowledge Progression Update”](https://steamcommunity.com/games/977400/announcements/detail/686380477674684636).

**Proposed for Rocket Raja.** Audit whether current prestige math or perks reward waiting through a long dead period more than resetting and playing another short run. A prestige preview should make the next reward's marginal benefit and likely time to it visible. If adding a permanent multiplier to reputation gain, model its effect on already-earned-but-unclaimed rewards carefully: “upgrade now” should not create a hidden penalty relative to waiting. Keep the *active decision* beneficial without making short-session players feel compelled to check in every few minutes.

**Tradeoff to test.** Retroactive changes accelerate progression sharply unless related cost and multiplier curves are rebalanced; Cell to Singularity paired the change with smaller Time Chamber factors. Any Rocket Raja prototype should simulate at least the first several prestiges and late Legacy ranks, not only the first reset.

## 6. Cookie Clicker: add jobs to familiar generators before adding more

**Developer-confirmed direction.** Orteil's 2023 version 2.052 announcement adds the twentieth building and another tier of upgrades and achievements, then says future work is planned around **minigames on existing buildings** rather than more buildings. Its official Steam achievement list separately rewards building ownership, cursor production, per-building production, ascensions, and golden-cookie interaction. That creates multiple goals that can bring attention back to an old generator even after its direct output is surpassed. [Orteil's announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1454400/announcements/detail/3716076292770968956); [official achievement list](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/1454400/achievements).

**Community-observed detail, lower confidence.** A 2021 player guide for version 2.031 describes Grandma and cross-building synergy upgrades, including requirements to own both building types. That supports the design pattern, but its exact percentages and unlock levels may differ today and should not become Rocket Raja tuning numbers. [Version-labeled community guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2591407770).

**Proposed for Rocket Raja.** Give two or three early firework families new *uses* as the stall expands: a support upgrade that benefits a later family, a visually distinct milestone, or a brief mini objective that uses that family. Preserve the satisfaction of unlocking a high-tier product. The goal is a periodic reason to invest in an old line, not an equal direct production share for all lines. Rewarding every family with dozens of repetitive levels would create busywork; a few authored interactions are easier to understand and balance.

**Tradeoff to test.** Achievement goals can motivate a purchase without making it economically good. Check whether a starter-family goal gives a visible outcome within the same session. If its only reward is a badge, it may fail to address the user's complaint that the product *feels irrelevant*.

## Cross-game synthesis for Rocket Raja

| Question to answer in a playtest | Mechanic worth prototyping | Direct precedent | Failure mode to watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Why revisit a starter family? | A small number of visible cross-family or whole-stall thresholds, plus selected late upgrades | AdVenture Capitalist cross-business Unlocks; Cookie Clicker existing-building expansion | Forced equal-level chores; newest product no longer feels exciting |
| Why tap after passive production accelerates? | A bounded link between *normal* product output and manual-tap payout, with tap-specific mastery | Realm Grinder tap-oriented faction and scalable click stat; Tap Titans 2 active build | Auto taps or skill stacks dominate; hand fatigue; active path becomes mandatory |
| Why prestige now? | Show reward, retained capability, expected rebuild, next unlock, and marginal gain from waiting | Tap Titans 2 first-prestige guide/starting stage; Idle Miner cost disclosure | Forecast seems like a promise; weak/no visible change in the next run |
| Why play the next run? | A small permanent specialization and one changing run goal | Realm Grinder factions/reincarnations; Idle Miner parallel objectives | Too many currencies and menus before first loop is understood |
| What should offline play do? | Recover established progress while active choices still open the frontier | Tap Titans 2 offline stage limit; AdVenture Capitalist permanent multipliers applying online and offline | Hard cap feels punitive; forced check-ins |
| Why return weeks later? | New goals on familiar systems, then measured additional depth | Cookie Clicker minigames on existing buildings; Tap Titans 2 season milestones | Content treadmill while basic pacing remains broken |

First repair the current baseline and make upgrade and Legacy choices visible, as described in the [comprehensive audit](Comprehensive%20Design%20Audit.md). Then compare a bounded shop-to-tap link, cross-family or portfolio milestones, and a prestige preview with one persistent choice—separately and together. If the first prestige still takes roughly 46 minutes in the current simulator, record how each change moves that time and how much income comes from starter families and tapping at several checkpoints. A benchmark describes a solution pattern; Rocket Raja's own traces and player comprehension determine whether it works.
