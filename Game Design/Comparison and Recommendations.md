# Tap Tycoon and Rocket Raja: design comparison

Research date: 30 September 2026. This is a design diagnosis and proposal, not a claim that Tap Tycoon's proprietary balance numbers are known. Read [Tap Tycoon Design Breakdown](Tap%20Tycoon%20Design%20Breakdown.md), [Rocket Raja Economy Audit](Rocket%20Raja%20Economy%20Audit.md), and [Research Sources](Research%20Sources.md) for evidence and limits.

## Executive assessment

Rocket Raja already has a functioning incremental loop: eight fireworks families, milestones, 96 cash upgrades, three active skills (two timed, one instant), and 12 permanent Legacy perks. A saved deterministic report lists first prestige at 24:35 and rebuild at 9:18, but a fresh run of the current code and exported catalog instead reaches them at **46:26 and 12:36**, and the simulator fails its pacing assertion. These are model results, not proof that a human understands or enjoys the reset. The user's three observations point to **how the systems connect**, more than to a simple shortage of multipliers.

Tap Tycoon's most transferable ideas are: early businesses feed all-business milestones; named upgrades revisit them across many orders of magnitude; late tapping gains a component linked to business output; and prestige links permanent income power with Tech Cards and weekly country contribution. The store sources corroborate the broad [tap/business/country loop](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gamehivecorp.tapmoney) and the historical addition of [cards and groups](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/tap-tycoon-country-vs-country/id1058709520). Shared milestones, repeated upgrade values, and Transforming come from **2015–2017 community observations**, not official balance tables. They do not prove Tap Tycoon is optimally tuned today. A [2016 player review](https://throwgame.wordpress.com/2016/02/06/tap-tycoon/) also reports weak ordinary tapping between skill bursts and a slow first prestige; a [2024 Google Play review](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gamehivecorp.tapmoney) similarly calls the first prestige lengthy.

## Mechanic-by-mechanic comparison

| Design question | Tap Tycoon evidence | Rocket Raja today | Diagnosis and direction |
|---|---|---|---|

The [2017 unlockables sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uGdqmtUNIKK3iJT1fyPMoQmZakOyXPX0GFkg0UqlMes/edit) revisits Restaurant at 40K, 13.1M, 4.3B, 25T, and much later. | Each family has its *own* doubling milestones and six profit upgrades. There is no cross-family level threshold or production synergy. Each successive base output is 8× the prior family. | Add a modest portfolio milestone and refresh selected early-family upgrades later in a run. Preserve choices: old-family purchases should compete with a current-family purchase on marginal portfolio return. |

A [2016 Transforming analysis](https://throwgame.wordpress.com/2016/06/23/tap-tycoon-transforming/) reports that tap profit gains a component equal to 1% of business profit after tap level 5,000; its time basis and exact equation are unverified. Active skills can stack. | Tap profit is a separate level formula multiplied by tap run upgrades and tap Legacy. The three are useful but mostly separate economic paths. | Add one understandable combo, such as a small tap-link boost during Festival Rush, with a hard cap and clear UI feedback. |

It does not show a numerical rebuild forecast or an upcoming permanent unlock. | Keep the existing disclosure and add contribution, next milestone, and “next run” outcomes where decisions happen. |

Legacy is a fixed 12-perk tree. | Extend meaningful goals in small slices. ## 1. Keep starting products relevant

### What the current math says

[Catalog.cs](../Assets/Game/Core/Catalog.cs#L67) scales each family by 8× in both initial cost and production. With one unit of all eight and no upgrades, Celebration boxes provide **87.5%** of passive output; Phuljhari supplies **0.0000417%**. These shares are a property of the geometric ladder, not a player sample. At the same level and multiplier, the marginal payback ratio is the same for every family because the 8× factors cancel. So an old family may still occasionally be an efficient purchase when its level is lower or it crosses a milestone, while its visible contribution can remain negligible. Both are important: the player needs a **reason to choose it** and a **visible result**.

[ProductMilestones.cs](../Assets/Game/Core/ProductMilestones.cs#L8) doubles each family's output at levels 25, 50, 100, and each further hundred. Those are self-contained. Tap Tycoon's reported all-business thresholds change the marginal value of an early business because buying it can increase every family's production. Its recurring Restaurant upgrades provide a second, direct reason to revisit the starter tier. Neither system guarantees equal raw income between early and late businesses, and equal income should not be the goal: later unlocks still need to feel like a jump.

### Recommended design

1. **Prototype portfolio milestones.** At thresholds such as every family reaching 25, 50, and 100, grant a modest run-wide production bonus. Prototype cumulative factors of 1.2×, 1.2×, and 1.3×; these are test inputs, not shipping values. Show the cheapest path to the next threshold and the projected portfolio gain. Avoid a requirement that forces eight identical purchases at every step with no competing strategy.
2. **Refresh early-family upgrades later.** Add a few late cash-price upgrades for Phuljhari, Anar, and Chakri, tied to a new visual/economic beat rather than endlessly repeating ×2. Some may raise their output; others can make them suppliers for the whole stall (for example, a small global boost from Phuljhari milestones). Use the authored catalog so exact prices and bonuses can be simulated.
3. **Expose contribution and opportunity cost.** On the product screen, show its share of current ₹/second, next milestone, and the expected gain from the next purchase. For a global milestone, show the total portfolio effect instead of only the old family's own rate.

**Guardrail:** Measure both direct income share and *marginal portfolio gain per rupee*. A small direct share is acceptable when the early family creates a meaningful cross-family benefit. Do not inflate every starter family's raw income until the newest unlock loses its impact.

## 2. Make tapping worth doing beyond the opening

Rocket Raja's tap rule is `(1 + 1.4 × tapLevel) × purchased tap multipliers × tap Legacy`; product profit is a separate multiplicative chain ([GameEngine.cs](../Assets/Game/Core/GameEngine.cs#L35)). As an illustrative state, tap level 60 and all 12 ×2 tap upgrades produce ₹348,160 per tap. At three taps per second that is ₹1,044,480/s. One level-one Celebration box with all eight ×2 all-product upgrades yields ₹1,503,238,553.6/s, making those taps roughly **0.0695%** of that one product's production. This is not a simulated play trace, but it demonstrates the scale separation possible in the current rules. Festival Rush further widens it while active.

Tap Tycoon's historically reported Transforming mechanic addresses this by using business profit as an input to tap profit. Its exact level-5,000 and 1% values are not suitable to copy without testing, and its own player review says routine taps later felt weak outside skill windows. The transferable idea is the **link**, with a meaningful active-play target and an explicit cap.

Prototype a “Showmanship” unlock after the economy is established:

`tap payout = existing tap payout + (linked share × normal product ₹/second ÷ reference taps/second)`

Start with a linked share around **8%** at a reference **3 manual taps/second**, then test 8–15% active-session contribution at the current mid-run frontier. The linked component should use *normal* product income, excluding ad payouts, offline rewards, and temporary Festival multipliers. A distinct tap mastery upgrade can raise the linked share in bounded steps; applying the existing twelve ×2 Salesmanship multipliers directly to the link could cause explosive growth. Cap the number of credited taps per second for this linked component, especially during Helping Hands. The exact cap and unlock timing belong in the balance simulation and human tests.

This creates two motivations: product growth keeps each tap current, and tap investment can improve the active route. The UI should break the payout into “tap level/bonuses” and “showmanship from shop” so the improvement is understandable. If normal play still awards under 5% of active-session earnings from tapping, consider an additional short burst or goal tied to deliberate taps, rather than multiplying all taps without bound.

## 3. Give prestige a stronger payoff and destination

Rocket Raja's first prestige is mechanically substantial. The current formula grants five reputation exactly at the ₹170Q threshold, and the fresh active trace still shows a faster rebuild (**46:26** before reset, **12:36** afterward); the older [Balance.md](../Documentation/Balance.md) numbers are stale for the current code. The first five points can buy the three-point all-product perk and two one-point perks. Therefore a blanket multiplier increase is not the first fix: the issue may be **choice, feedback, or novelty**, alongside a real first-run pacing problem. Human testing has not yet confirmed that players notice the faster rebuild.

The reset should answer three questions before confirmation: “What will I gain now?”, “What will my next run feel like?”, and “What longer goal did I advance?” The existing reset/keep cards already answer what is lost and retained ([BuildImportedPrestige.cs](../Assets/Game/Editor/BuildImportedPrestige.cs#L97)). Add a projected early-run product/tap rate using the chosen Legacy purchase, an approximate rebuild time clearly labeled as a model, and the next permanent unlock. Never promise an exact time for a human player's behavior.

For new permanent depth, prototype a small **Legacy choice** earned at a prestige milestone, with three paths: a portfolio effect, an active tap effect, or a skill/offline effect. Make the choice visible before resetting and preserve a respec path so early choices do not trap players. Prefer deterministic choices or a disclosed shop to random card drops; the historical Tap Tycoon sources show persistent cards, but a [2016 player account](https://throwgame.wordpress.com/2016/02/06/tap-tycoon/) also describes bad early draws. A later source of small, diminishing permanent progression is needed once the fixed 434-reputation perk budget is complete, but add it only after observing whether players actually reach that ceiling.

Prestige should also offer optional run goals that vary the replay: for example, a portfolio milestone, a tap-use objective, or a timed skill objective. These should grant a modest permanent currency or cosmetic/stall evolution without making one play style mandatory. A country-war-sized social system is far beyond the present scope; a local rotating goal can test the same “a reason for the next run” principle first.

## Priorities

| Priority | Change | Why first | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Resolve the failing pacing assertion; record current active income shares, product purchases, and prestige comprehension | Establishes a trustworthy baseline and distinguishes a weak formula from an invisible reward or goal | Simulator behavior is not a substitute for human behavior |
| P1 | Bounded business-to-tap link and UI breakdown | Directly repairs the tap path after products dominate | Auto taps or existing ×2 upgrades could amplify it too far |
| P1 | Portfolio milestone at a few thresholds, with visible next goal | Gives starter families a global economic role | Run acceleration can erase the current 20–30 minute first prestige target |
| P1 | Prestige before/after forecast and first permanent specialization | Gives reset a perceivable consequence | Save compatibility and overpowered stacking |
| P2 | Late cash upgrades and visual goals for starter families | Creates periodic return to old products | Filler upgrades can become mandatory chores |
| P3 | Long-tail prestige sink and rotating goals | Extends play after the fixed Legacy tree | Premature complexity before the core loop is validated |

See [Balance Experiment Plan](Balance%20Experiment%20Plan.md) for candidate formulas, measures, and acceptance checks. These are design proposals; this research task does not alter the live economy.
