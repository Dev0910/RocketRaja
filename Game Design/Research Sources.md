# Tap Tycoon research sources and evidence log

Research date: 30 September 2026. This document concerns **Game Hive Corporation's _Tap Tycoon: Country vs Country_** (Android package `com.gamehivecorp.tapmoney`; iOS app ID `1058709520`). Several unrelated browser clickers and the Telegram game TAPCO also use “Tap Tycoon” language; none of their figures are evidence for Game Hive's game.

## How to read the evidence

- **Official listing / developer record:** strongest evidence for the game's identity, intended loop, release history, and published changes. Store descriptions do not reveal balance formulas.
- **Contemporary play report:** useful evidence for mechanics a player encountered in a specific version, especially when independently corroborated. Some numbers changed after launch.
- **Fan-maintained data:** detailed enough to form a balance hypothesis, but it is not a developer export or verified current build.
- **Player review:** evidence about a player's experience or pain point, not a representative measure of all players.

The public material supports the structure of Tap Tycoon's balance more strongly than it supports its exact formulas. In particular, no verified business cost curve, base payout table, or prestige-point equation was located. Do not present proposed formulas for those as Game Hive's implementation.

## Source-code and raw-data search

Searches for `"Tap Tycoon" "Game Hive" github source`, `"com.gamehivecorp.tapmoney" github`, `"Tap Tycoon" "GameHive" source code GitHub`, and balance-data / CSV variants did **not** surface an official Game Hive source repository, licensed source release, or official balance configuration. The public 2017 unlockables spreadsheet above is a **fan transcription**, not game source code. Search results also mixed in unrelated games such as TAPCO, ZeroPlay's browser game, and Tycoon Cards; their formulas are not Tap Tycoon formulas. No APK was downloaded or decompiled for this research. The defensible finding is **“no official source or raw balance data found in the searched public sources,”** not proof that none exists anywhere.

## Findings that deserve verification before copying numbers

1. The exact business purchase-cost and payout curves remain unknown from the sources above. The unlockables sheet documents upgrade prices and multipliers only.
2. Multiple early guides put first prestige near $20 trillion, but the prestige-point function and later eligibility rule need live-build verification. The $20 trillion figure is historical.
3. The `1.26×` soldier rule appears in older posts and is explicitly retracted in a later guide. Do not use it for a design formula.
4. “Transforming” at tap level 5000 and the 1% business-to-tap bridge are detailed community observations; useful as a design pattern, not an official current coefficient.
5. Stronger milestone synergy and recurring restaurant / tap unlockables show **reasons to revisit** old businesses and active play. They do not by themselves prove any target percentage of income contribution.
