06 / Evidence boundaries

What still needs real players

Use this dashboard to choose investigations, not to declare retention or fun solved.

Read full limitations ↗

Simulated policy ≠ average player

The 46:26 run assumes relentless three-tap input and automated purchases. Low-tap, check-in, offline, and sponsor policies may follow different paths. Measurement plan ↗

Screen evidence ≠ comprehension

Code and desktop captures show what is available or hidden, but only uncoached sessions can establish what players notice, understand, or prefer. Playtest sheet ↗

Precedent ≠ causal proof

Research and competitor mechanics suggest hypotheses. No reviewed paper gives a universal tap share, product distribution, prestige time, or Rocket Raja retention effect. Research limits ↗
