# Draft store listing

Title: Rocket Raja

Short description: Grow a festive fireworks stall. Tap, upgrade your shelves and build your Legacy.

Start with an empty stall and fill it with festive fireworks. Tap to earn rupees, introduce familiar products such as Phuljhari, Anar and Chakri, and reach profit milestones as your shop grows.

Your shop earns automatically. Choose upgrades, activate Festival Rush, call on Helping Hands or fulfill a Bulk Order. When your shop is ready, prestige for reputation and invest in permanent Legacy perks to build faster next time.

- Eight fireworks product families with economic level milestones and shelf artwork.
- 96 upgrades to discover during each run.
- Three active skills and 12 permanent Legacy perks.
- Offline earnings and local saving.
- Optional rewarded videos; no forced ad breaks.
- Music, sound and reduced-motion settings.

Play throughout the year. This is a fictional retail game with no purchases or real fireworks for sale. Progress is stored on your device; cloud recovery is not available.
